About Me

I am a Senior Performance Engineer with over 10 years of experience architecting end-to-end Non-Functional Testing (NFT) strategies, distributed system scalability, and enterprise resiliency across the Banking, Telecom, and Energy sectors.

Currently at American Express (Senior Engineer II), I lead performance initiatives for Payment Network Authorization systems—scaling processing capacity to 6,000+ Transactions Per Second (TPS). Recently, I designed and built an internal AI Performance Agent using LangGraph & RAG that automates test execution, parses multi-dimensional telemetry, and enables conversational root-cause analysis of historical performance bottlenecks.

My technical focus bridges traditional deep-stack performance engineering (JVM heap/thread profiling, network latency emulation with Toxiproxy, chaos resiliency testing) with modern cloud-native architectures (Kubernetes, OpenShift, Kafka, AWS) and agentic AI tooling. In recognition of these contributions, I was awarded the American Express Centurion Award 2025.
